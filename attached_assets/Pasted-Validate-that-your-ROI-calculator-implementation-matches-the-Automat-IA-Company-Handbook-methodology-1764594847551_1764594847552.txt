effectiveHourlyRate = (annualSalary / annualWorkingHours) × overheadMultiplier

YEAR 1 RAMP-UP CALCULATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
YEAR 1 RAMP FACTOR (for ramp-up periods ≤12 months):
year1RampFactor = (rampUpMonths × 0.5 + (12 - rampUpMonths) × 1.0) / 12
Business rule: During ramp-up months, assume 50% average efficiency. After ramp-up, assume 100%.
PAYBACK PERIOD (month-by-month tracking):
For each month 1-12:
  monthEfficiency = (month <= rampUpMonths) ? 0.5 : 1.0
  monthlyBenefit = (annualBenefits / 12) × monthEfficiency
  cumulativeBenefits += monthlyBenefit
  if (cumulativeBenefits >= year1TotalCosts) → paybackMonth found

ROI METRICS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
YEAR 1 ACTUAL ROI (with ramp-up):
year1ActualROI = ((year1Benefits - year1Costs) / year1Costs) × 100
where year1Benefits = runRateBenefits × year1RampFactor
ANNUAL RUN-RATE ROI (steady-state, no ramp-up):
runRateROI = ((annualBenefits - annualCosts) / annualCosts) × 100
