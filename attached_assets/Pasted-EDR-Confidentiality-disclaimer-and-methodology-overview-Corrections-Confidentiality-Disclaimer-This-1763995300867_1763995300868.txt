Phase 3: Process Insights Form (PIF)
PIFs are collected at the step level—each individual process step within a workflow receives its own streamlined PIF assessment. The form consists of up to 29 questions (depending on conditional logic), primarily multiple-choice with only 3 numeric inputs required from respondents. Operational participants provide quantitative data including process clarity scores, change readiness indicators, workflow brittleness metrics, time/volume data, and quality impact measurements. This focused, quantitative approach minimizes survey fatigue while capturing standardized operational readiness scores and accurate ROI modeling inputs at the step level, enabling precise automation opportunity identification without relying on aggregated workflow averages.

This section:
7.4 Standard vs. Enhanced Opportunity Matrix
Automat-IA applies a Two-Matrix Framework:
1.     The Base Impact Opportunity Matrix calculates Business Impact based on Annual ROI percentage and FTE savings, providing initial prioritization that supports executive visibility and quick sequencing of opportunities.
2.     The Enhanced Opportunity Matrix applies an additive bonus model to the Business Impact axis only, expanding the scale from 0-100 to 0-150 for opportunities that demonstrate additional value through error reduction or revenue uplift. This refined matrix adjusts opportunity rankings to reflect strategic interdependencies, ensuring a balanced portfolio of short-term gains and sustainable long-term automation value.

7.4 Standard vs. Enhanced Opportunity Matrix
