2. Quality Impact Calculation (Manual Mode)
Use this formula for adjusting error reduction based on scenario:

rawErrorReduction = currentErrorRate - postAIErrorRate
errorReduction = rawErrorReduction × scenario.errorReductionMultiplier
errorReduction = MIN(errorReduction, currentErrorRate)  // Cap at current rate
adjustedPostAIErrorRate = currentErrorRate - errorReduction
errorsAvoidedAnnually = (errorReduction / 100) × transactionsPerWeek × 52

DO NOT use: postAIErrorRate × (2 - multiplier)

3. Revenue Uplift Default
When revenueValuePerHour is 0 or not set, fall back to effectiveHourlyRate (calculated from salary), NOT a fixed $85:

revenuePerHour = (revenueValuePerHour > 0) ? revenueValuePerHour : effectiveHourlyRate

4. Payback Period Calculation
Use steady-state annual benefits (not ramp-adjusted Year 1 benefits):

paybackMonths = year1Costs / (annualBenefits / 12)

Where:

year1Costs = total Year 1 costs (implementation + software + training + change mgmt) × costOverrunMultiplier
annualBenefits = steady-state annual benefits at 100% capacity (no ramp-up applied)
5. Year 1 Ramp Factor Formula
baseRampFactor = (12 - rampUpMonths + (rampUpMonths / 2)) / 12
scenarioAdjustedEfficiency = MAX(20, MIN(150, rampUpEfficiencyPercent × scenario.rampUpEfficiencyMultiplier))
year1RampFactor = baseRampFactor × (scenarioAdjustedEfficiency / 100)
year1Benefits = annualBenefits × year1RampFactor
