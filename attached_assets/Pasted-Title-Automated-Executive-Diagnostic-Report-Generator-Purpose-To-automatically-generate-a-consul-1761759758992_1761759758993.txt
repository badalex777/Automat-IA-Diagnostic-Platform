Title:
 Automated Executive Diagnostic Report Generator
Purpose:
 To automatically generate a consulting-grade Executive Diagnostic Report (Word document) for each company once at least one full TFA and one PIF have been completed and at least one opportunity has been positioned on the Opportunity Matrix.
Instruction:
 When the “Generate Report” button is clicked for a company profile, the platform must assemble all relevant diagnostic data and produce a formatted .docx report that mirrors the official Automat-IA Executive Diagnostic Report Template (v1 – Oct 2025).

1. Input Conditions
A company profile must exist and include:


Financial context (annual revenue, total employees)


At least one workflow with a complete TFA and complete PIF


At least one opportunity plotted on the Opportunity Matrix


System data required:


Readiness Rubric outputs (overall and per workflow)


ROI Calculator results (annual benefit, payback, ROI %)


Opportunity Matrix data (impact vs effort coordinates)


Roadmap phases (Quick Wins, Mid-Term, Long-Term)


Audit trail (respondent names, roles, submission dates, and any knowledge-gap resolutions)



2. Output Specifications
Generate a Word document (.docx) containing all sections of the official report with:
Auto-filled company-specific data


Editable narrative placeholders marked by [brackets] where human interpretation is required


Document Structure (Auto-generated)
Cover Page


Executive Diagnostic Report for [Company Name]


Diagnostic Period = [Start Date – End Date]


Analyst = Alejandro Madrid-Malo


Version = v1 – [Month Year]


Executive Summary


Autogenerate quantitative highlights:


[X workflows analyzed], [Y opportunities identified]


Total annual benefit $[X,XXX,XXX]


Average payback [X months]


Conservative ROI [XXX %]


Insert editable narrative fields:


[Key strengths and weaknesses summary]


[High-feasibility Quick Wins summary]


[Strategic narrative on scalability and next-phase potential]


Confidentiality & Disclaimer


Insert standardized legal and methodological disclaimer (verbatim from template).


Methodology Overview


Insert condensed, executive-friendly summary of the Automat-IA methodology:


Data Sources (TFA + PIF + ROI Calculator + Matrix)


Framework references (TFA = 60% Tech, PIF = 40% Ops)


Conservative Scenario Framework note


Provide optional [Detailed Methodology Appendix] toggle for full version.


Diagnostic Overview


Auto-populate from webapp data:


4.1 Scope of Evaluation → list of analyzed workflows


4.2 Key Findings → include readiness average and ROI range


4.3 Top 3 Opportunities → pull top 3 by ROI


4.4 Financial Summary → insert from ROI Calculator

Opportunity Matrix: insert both Base and Enhanced Matrix images or tables


Automation Roadmap

Insert editable [Narrative Summary] section

 “Y foundational improvements required before scaling”
 “Z strategic initiatives planned for long-term ROI”


Add placeholders:


[Strategic interpretation and client-specific insights]


[Next steps for executive presentation or workshop]


Appendices (Full Audit Trail)


Appendix 1: Readiness SWOT (per opportunity)


Appendix 2: Metric weighting and category interpretation


Appendix 3: ROI details (all inputs and outputs)


Appendix 4: ROI calculation methodology (from ROI Handbook)


Appendix 5: Data Provenance and Audit Trail
 (TFA respondent, PIF respondent, dates, knowledge gap notes)


Appendix 6: [Optional Knowledge Gaps & Recommendations]



3. Formatting & Quality Rules
Must mirror Automat-IA’s consulting-grade tone: concise, analytical, risk-adjusted.


Use consistent heading hierarchy and professional formatting (Roboto 11, 1.15 spacing).


All quantitative data must use Conservative Scenario Framework assumptions.


Each auto-filled metric must cite its diagnostic source (TFA, PIF, ROI Calc, Matrix).


Editable sections clearly indicated by [brackets] for post-generation human review.


Include auto-generated Table of Contents and page headers/footers with
 “Prepared for [Client] by Automat-IA | Confidential”.



4. Tone & Output Goal
The autogenerated draft should be 90–95% complete, ready for analyst review.


Only strategic commentary, interpretive insights, and client-specific narratives require manual editing.


Report must be boardroom-ready in structure and clarity while preserving analytical depth.
