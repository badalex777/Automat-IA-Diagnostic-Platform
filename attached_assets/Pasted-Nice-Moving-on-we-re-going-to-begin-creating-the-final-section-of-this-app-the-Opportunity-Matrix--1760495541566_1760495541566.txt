Nice. Moving on—we’re going to begin creating the final section of this app: the Opportunity Matrix. Before we dive in, it’s important to understand the sequence that leads to this stage.
After stakeholder interviews, where we identify the best candidate workflows for automation, we first send the Technical Feasibility Assessment (TFA) to the system managers of those workflows. This grounds us in the technical reality and context before investing time in workflows that aren’t suitable—or would require significant groundwork before reaching optimal automation.
Once TFA data is collected, we distribute Process Insight Forms (PIFs) to end-users (the employees whose work will be made easier through automation/AI). These responses feed into the Readiness Rubric, helping us understand the workflow’s mechanics in detail. At this stage, we can accurately model the Y-axis (Implementation Effort) of the Opportunity Matrix.
After gathering TFA and PIF data, we conduct end-user interviews. These uncover real inefficiencies, error-prone steps, and bottlenecks within each candidate workflow. From these insights, we begin brainstorming potential AI automations for the designated workflownd based on  the data collected in PIFs and our own estimates of implementation costs, training, and change management for potential automations within the workflow, we begin ROI modeling, The process looks like this:
Analyse feasibility and confirm workflow readiness for automation.


Brainstorm automation/AI implementations within each workflow.


Model ROI around those implementations, drawing on both PIF data and our cost/effort estimates.


Important note: multiple end-users from different roles within each workflow may fill out PIFs. Their data, role, and answers will guide us in brainstorming specific AI implementations for each part of the process. And as their data and insights from the PIF and interviews will be different, we will be able to accurately brainstorm and suggest potential automations for each section of the workflow that might have inefficiencies that AI could help mitigate.
